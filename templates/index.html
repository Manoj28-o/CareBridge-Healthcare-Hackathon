Good to know

Is this a real hospital service?

No. It is a hackathon demo. All doctors and patients are fictional, and no SMS or email is sent.

Do I need an account?

You can browse doctors freely. You need a patient account to book an appointment.

How is my login protected?

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as plain text, and sessions use a protected cookie.
